  hard rule); the other is unchanged (115 of 115 allowed before and after).
- **Favored foods moving from `allowed` to `unverified`: 0** (no account has starred Favorites yet).

not a reason to read Jev again.

## Follow-up

Option B (ingredient parser, `lib/policy/diet-label.ts`) is a separate change with its own review.
It would parse the ingredient statement apart from Contains clauses, split at the right parenthesis
depth, treat "and"/"or" as joins, and require every generic head to carry its own local plant
source. The shared label-completeness lexicon is not widened for W2c.
