Verdict: DO NOT SHIP — 2 BLOCK, 1 FIX, 0 NIT

1. [BLOCK] **The Round 12 refusal path can strand newer settings edits.** The spec says to remove an `onboarding_stamp_locked` op and call `settleUserSettings(null, pull)` (`docs/specs/phase-10-conversational-onboarding.md:813-817`). A null settlement does not clear the frozen op’s `sent` marker (`lib/sync/settings.ts:231-262`; `lib/sync/settings-intents.ts:319-335`). The next send sees that marker and skips freezing the successor (`lib/sync/settings.ts:155-173`). Require a settlement that takes the refused op, discards only its generation, clears `sent`, preserves later intents, and reconciles to the fetched server row. Test a newer edit queued while the refused op is in flight.

2. [BLOCK] **The Gemini health gate also admits disclosure to Jev.** Phase 10 sends onboarding text to Jev to classify conditions and allergens, using `routeFor` as the gate (`docs/specs/phase-10-conversational-onboarding.md:356-373`). That gate authorizes a Google model when the Gemini billing flags are set (`lib/ai/route.ts:48-55`); `systemOne` separately posts its `state` to TypeSafe (`lib/ai/typesafe.ts:10,255-256,191-200`). Specify a separate approval rule for health data sent to Jev, or keep health text and fields out of its request. Test the actual outbound Jev body after a condition disclosed at stage 1.

3. [FIX] **A saved name with no food ref has no usable reader.** Phase 10 tells users it saved unmatched Eats and Avoids (`docs/specs/phase-10-conversational-onboarding.md:880-890`), but `set_food_membership` then writes only `food_membership_revisions` (`supabase/migrations/20261023090400_food_membership_v2.sql:25-56,93-123`). Foods builds its lists from `user_foods` (`lib/store.ts:274-311`; `components/MyFoods.tsx:91-103`), and ordinary coach context reads `user_food_prefs` (`lib/server/coach-context.ts:129-146`). Give these pending names an owner-scoped, visible path for review and adding details, or change the hand-off so it does not claim they are saved in Foods.
