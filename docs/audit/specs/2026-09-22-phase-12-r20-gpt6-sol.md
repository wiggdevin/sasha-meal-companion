[BLOCK] **Atomic Undo can restore the wrong weigh-in.** The proposed RPC takes `p_previous_kg` and checks the target revision, but does not identify or version-check the manual sample it will replace ([Phase 12:322](/Users/zero-suminc./projects/clients/Sasha/meal-companion/docs/specs/phase-12-coach-tools.md:322)).
