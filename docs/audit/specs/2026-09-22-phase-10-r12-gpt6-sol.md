Verdict: DO NOT SHIP — 1 BLOCK, 1 FIX, 0 NIT

1. [BLOCK] **A second device can overwrite the approved time zone after completion.** The spec saves the summary’s zone in `user_settings`, but its stamp trigger allows later writes that leave `onboarded_at` unchanged (`docs/specs/phase-10-conversational-onboarding.md:665-670,743-746,800-814`). `AuthGate` queues `captureTimeZone()` even for an unfinished account (`components/AuthGate.tsx:299-310`); that op can contain only the second device’s zone (`lib/sync/settings.ts:96-115`). If it drains after another device completes onboarding, settings recovery replays that zone onto the completed row (`lib/sync/settings-intents.ts:226-233,360-364`), and `_sync_upsert` accepts it (`supabase/migrations/20261026090100_neutral_user_defaults.sql:179-181`). The pre-completion flush covers only the completing device. Stop automatic zone capture for unfinished `default` accounts, or fence and retire those pending zone intents. Test two devices in different zones, with the second device draining after completion.

2. [FIX] **The promised recovery control for a refused settings op has no defined implementation.** The spec says `onboarding_stamp_locked` parks the entry and Sync Status offers **Discard** (`docs/specs/phase-10-conversational-onboarding.md:800-828`). The current outbox retries an error three times before parking it (`lib/sync/outbox.ts:194-209`), while Sync Status shows only a pending count and **Sync now**, which releases parked ops for another retry (`components/SyncStatus.tsx:28-73,134-143`). Specify a recognizable permanent refusal, a targeted Discard action, and reconciliation of the local settings mirror when it is discarded; test the visible status and recovery path.
