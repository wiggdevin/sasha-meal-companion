### BLOCK

1. **[BLOCK] `contains_allergen:<name>`** — Its `false` rubric treats no named ingredient and no “may contain” warning as absence. For non-major allergens, ingredients can appear under generic names, and precautionary warnings are voluntary. A negative Jev answer must not clear a named allergy; keep insufficient evidence `unverified`. [FDA labeling guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/guidance-industry-questions-and-answers-regarding-food-allergen-labeling-edition-5), [FDA on voluntary warnings](https://www.fda.gov/food/conversations-experts-food-topics/current-food-allergen-landscape).

2. **[BLOCK] `allergy_tree_nut`, `allergy_crustacean`** — “Allergic to nuts” maps to tree nuts alone, and “shellfish allergy” maps to crustaceans alone. Either phrase can leave the other food group unblocked. Ask which foods are implicated before narrowing the rule; keep an unresolved report unverified. [FDA food-allergen categories](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/food-allergies).

3. **[BLOCK] `allergy_milk.baked_milk_ok`, `allergy_egg.baked_egg_ok`** — “Baked goods allowed” is broader than a passed baked-milk or baked-egg challenge. Clearance applies to the allergist’s specified recipe, matrix, dose, and progression; other baked products remain unverified. [AAAAI baked-milk/egg guide](https://education.aaaai.org/sites/default/files/media/2024-11/Muffins%20%26%20More%20Guidebook_Online%20Version.pdf).

4. **[BLOCK] `oral_allergy_syndrome`** — Every named raw trigger is only `soft`. Pollen-food allergy can cause systemic reactions, including anaphylaxis. Ask about reactions and allergist instructions; a specifically confirmed unsafe form needs a hard user-confirmed rule. [ACAAI](https://acaai.org/allergies/allergic-conditions/food/pollen-food-allergy-syndrome/).

5. **[BLOCK] `celiac`, `contains_gluten`** — The map notes an oats/brewer’s-yeast rubric fix but does not supply a rule that prevents an ingredient-list negative from clearing these products. Oats can carry gluten through cross-contact; high-risk processed foods need gluten-free verification or an `unverified` result. [NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/celiac-disease/eating-diet-nutrition), [FDA gluten-free rule guidance](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/questions-and-answers-gluten-free-food-labeling-final-rule).

6. **[BLOCK] `pku`** — Blocking aspartame and disabling protein nudges still leaves ordinary high-phenylalanine meals available in the planner. PKU planning needs the patient’s metabolic-clinic phenylalanine prescription and medical-food regimen; without them, do not generate an ordinary macro-based plan. [ACMG guideline, DOI:10.1016/j.gim.2024.101289](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gim.2024.101289).

7. **[BLOCK] `dysphagia`, `texture_level`** — Jev guesses one IDDSI level from a food description and that guess feeds a hard clearance. Food and drink levels are separate, and compliance depends on preparation and physical testing as served. Require the prescribed levels and verified preparation; otherwise return `unverified`. [IDDSI framework and testing methods](https://www.iddsi.org/resources/framework-documents).

8. **[BLOCK] `high_listeria_risk`** — The rubric implies pasteurized queso fresco is safe and omits premade deli salads, deli-sliced cheese, and fermented dry sausages. Unheated queso fresco-type cheese remains higher risk even when pasteurized; add CDC’s categories and 165°F/steaming-hot preparation rule. [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/listeria/prevention/index.html).

9. **[BLOCK] `pregnancy`, `gestational_diabetes`, `keto`, `low_carb`** — A pregnancy profile has no carbohydrate minimum while another active profile can set 20–50 g net or under 130 g/day. Pregnancy nutrition guidance specifies at least **175 g carbohydrate/day** and advises against a ketogenic pattern; resolve this conflict before producing targets. [ADA Standards of Care 2026](https://diabetesjournals.org/care/article/49/Supplement_1/S321/163918/15-Management-of-Diabetes-in-Pregnancy-Standards).

10. **[BLOCK] `ckd`** — A generic CKD report assigns **0.8 g/kg/day** protein without a stage. KDIGO’s recommendation is for **adults with G3–G5 CKD who are not on dialysis**, with exceptions for metabolic instability and undernutrition risk. Obtain stage and treatment context before changing the protein target. [KDIGO 2024, §3.3.1](https://kdigo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/KDIGO-2024-CKD-Guideline.pdf).

11. **[BLOCK] `bariatric_post_op`** — It says to follow the program’s texture stage but has no operative stage input or rule to prevent solid-food suggestions immediately after surgery. Require the bariatric team’s current liquid, puréed, soft, or solid stage before planning meals. [ASMBS](https://asmbs.org/patients/life-after-bariatric-surgery/).

12. **[BLOCK] `alcohol_interacting_drug`** — Disulfiram is represented by `alcohol` “as consumed” and a course-length window. Alcohol-containing sauces, medicines, and similar products matter, and reactions can occur for **up to 14 days after the last dose**. Give disulfiram its own ingredient and time-window rule. [FDA disulfiram label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/pre96/88482ap.pdf).

13. **[BLOCK] `bisphosphonate`** — The generic **30-minute** reminder cannot cover oral ibandronate, which requires **60 minutes** before food, other drinks, or oral medicines and remaining upright for 60 minutes. Match the exact medicine before issuing timing advice. [FDA Boniva label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2007/021455s005lbl.pdf).

14. **[BLOCK] `maoi` / missing `linezolid`** — Linezolid is absent from the medication map although its label warns against large amounts of high-tyramine foods because of a pressor response. Add a drug-specific linezolid rule; do not infer that its instructions are identical to chronic MAOI therapy. [FDA linezolid label, 2026](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/021130s050lbl.pdf).

15. **[BLOCK] `statin_grapefruit` / missing `tacrolimus`** — Grapefruit avoidance is mapped for selected statins but no transplant-drug profile covers tacrolimus. Its label says to avoid grapefruit or grapefruit juice. Add medication-specific recognition and an interaction rule. [FDA Prograf label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2021/050708s053%2C050709s045%2C210115s005lbl.pdf).

16. **[BLOCK] `ramadan_fasting` with `type1_diabetes` or `sglt2_inhibitor`** — The draft can compress daily targets into the fasting window while only listing these as conflicts. Do not generate a fasting-adjusted plan for these high-risk combinations without individual clinical risk assessment and a medication plan. [IDF-DAR 2026 risk update](https://idf.org/about-diabetes/resources/idf-dar-risk-calculator-update-2026/).

17. **[BLOCK] `general_health`, `fat_loss` / pediatric users** — The map applies adult weight-loss rates and **1,200/1,500 kcal** floors without an age gate, while the app accepts child birth years. Pediatric targets require an age-specific pathway; even the cited DGA sodium limit of **2,300 mg** starts at age 14. [AAP pediatric obesity guideline](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-060640), [DGA 2025–2030](https://cdn.realfood.gov/DGA.pdf).

18. **[BLOCK] `caffeine`, `high_sodium`, `high_potassium`, `high_phosphorus`** — Jev’s broad “typical serving” scores cannot enforce daily milligram limits or individualized renal budgets. Use measured nutrients and actual portions for arithmetic; with missing values, state that the limit cannot be checked. This matters especially for pregnancy’s **200 mg/day caffeine** limit. [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy), [KDIGO 2024](https://kdigo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/KDIGO-2024-CKD-Guideline.pdf).

### FIX

- **[FIX] `ckd_dialysis`, `glp1_medication`** — Dialysis automatically demotes high-potassium/phosphorus foods despite saying limits are lab-driven; GLP-1 protein goals have no CKD conflict. Activate mineral limits only from the care plan and resolve protein targets against renal treatment and nutrition status. [KDOQI nutrition guideline, DOI:10.1053/j.ajkd.2020.05.006](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.ajkd.2020.05.006).

- **[FIX] `alcohol_interacting_drug` / metronidazole** — Grouping metronidazole with disulfiram implies the same established alcohol reaction. Separate them: follow the prescribed product’s labeling, while acknowledging CDC’s finding that convincing evidence for a disulfiram-like metronidazole reaction is lacking. [CDC STI guidelines](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/70/rr/rr7004a1.htm).

- **[FIX] `fat_loss`** — **1.6–2.4 g/kg body weight** for all users is attributed partly to Helms, whose recommendation concerns lean, resistance-trained bodybuilding contestants and uses **fat-free mass**. The sex-based calorie floors are example adult plans, not universal safety thresholds. Individualize both claims and citations. [Helms, DOI:10.1186/1550-2783-11-20](https://doi.org/10.1186/1550-2783-11-20), [adult obesity guideline](https://doi.org/10.1161/01.cir.0000437739.71477.ee).

- **[FIX] `pregnancy`, `lactation`** — The **+340/+452** and **+330/+400 kcal** figures are described as prior-DGA estimates but the pregnancy entry cites the 2025–2030 DGA for them. Cite the **2020–2025 DGA** and retain its pregnancy-weight and breastfeeding assumptions rather than presenting fixed additions as individualized targets. [USDA/HHS 2020–2025 DGA](https://www.dietaryguidelines.gov/sites/default/files/2020-12/Dietary_Guidelines_for_Americans_2020-2025.pdf).

- **[FIX] `osteoporosis`** — **800–1,000 IU vitamin D** is stated without age or treatment context. The general RDA is **600 IU through age 70** and **800 IU after 70**; a higher clinical dose needs its own indication. [NIH ODS](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminD-HealthProfessional/).

- **[FIX] `heart_ldl`** — The cited 2021 AHA dietary statement has been replaced by the **2026** statement. Update the citation and review the profile’s LDL-versus-triglyceride synonym and target language against it. [AHA 2026, DOI:10.1161/CIR.0000000000001435](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001435).

- **[FIX] Missing `eosinophilic_esophagitis`; `low_fat_directive`** — EoE needs a distinct, monitored elimination-and-reintroduction pathway; it must not be treated as an IgE food allergy. The low-fat directive’s NCI cancer-nutrition citation does not substantiate a pancreatitis directive; add condition-specific guidance instead. [ACG EoE guideline, DOI:10.14309/ajg.0000000000003194](https://doi.org/10.14309/ajg.0000000000003194), [NICE pancreatitis guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng104/chapter/recommendations).

- **[FIX] `alpha_gal`, `gelatin`** — Hard-blocking every animal gelatin also blocks fish gelatin, which does not contain alpha-gal. Distinguish mammalian gelatin from verified fish-derived gelatin; leave unknown source unverified. [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/alpha-gal-syndrome/data-research/products-containing-alpha-gal/index.html).

- **[FIX] `whole30`, `added_sugar`, `beans`, `contains_dairy`** — A “high added sugar” score misses foods with small amounts of added sugar; the existing `beans` rubric calls hummus/refried beans false; `contains_dairy` blocks permitted ghee. Use ingredient-level program rules and its stated exceptions. [Official Whole30 rules](https://whole30.com/original-program-rules/).

- **[FIX] `fish_without_fins_scales` (`kosher`, `sda`)** — “Lacks **both** fins and scales” can return false for a fish that has fins but lacks acceptable scales. Ask whether the fish fails **either** required criterion, with source and community-specific exceptions. [OU Kosher fish criteria](https://oukosher.org/blog/industrial-kosher/fish-qa/).

- **[FIX] `halal`, `hindu_no_beef`, `gelatin`** — A universal gelatin hard block rejects certified halal gelatin; the Hindu “no cattle gelatin” option rejects fish gelatin. Record animal source and relevant certification, and ask when unknown. [IFANCA halal guidance](https://ifanca.org/app/uploads/2023/02/Islamic-Perspective-XV1.pdf), [Hindu American Foundation dietary examples](https://www.hinduamerican.org/employee-accommodation-request-dietary-restrictions).

- **[FIX] `kosher.level`** — Default `no_pork_shellfish` is not one of the declared choices, while the base hard list already enforces meat–dairy and other stricter rules. Make each selectable observance level a valid value with its own rule set; ask about certification rather than inferring it. [OU Kosher primer](https://oukosher.org/nav-test-kosher-food-the-kosher-primer/).

- **[FIX] `kosher.passover`** — `contains_grain → hard` treats rice and corn as chametz even without the kitniyot option. Chametz concerns the five specified grains; kitniyot is a separate, community-dependent practice. [OU Passover glossary](https://oukosher.org/passover/articles/glossary-of-common-passover-terms/).

- **[FIX] `lent_catholic`, existing `meat` facet** — `meat` includes broth, stock, and gelatin, so the Friday rule blocks foods the USCCB says are technically permitted. Also represent Ash Wednesday/Good Friday fasting separately from meat abstinence, with age and health exceptions. [USCCB on meat abstinence](https://www.usccb.org/es/node/50977), [USCCB fasting norms](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgical-year-and-calendar/lent/catholic-information-on-lenten-fast-and-abstinence).

- **[FIX] `orthodox_fasting`** — Its hard `fish_seafood` rule initially includes shellfish despite `shellfish_allowed: true`, and its citation is a lipid study rather than a fasting authority. Apply the user’s local calendar and practice, including fish, wine, and oil feast-day allowances; cite church guidance. [Orthodox Church in America](https://www.oca.org/liturgics/outlines/fasting-fast-free-seasons-of-the-church).

- **[FIX] `sikh.no_ritual_slaughter_meat`** — Requiring affirmative *jhatka* evidence for all meat goes beyond the cited Sikh Rehat Maryada’s prohibition of *kutha* meat. Ask the user’s observance and identify the prohibited slaughter method; do not silently require a jhatka certificate. [SGPC Rehat Maryada](https://old.sgpc.net/rehat_maryada/section_six.html).

- **[FIX] `caffeine_free`** — An explicit “caffeine-free” preference is represented only by a soft caffeine limit. Offer a confirmed `user_rule` hard option for “never caffeine,” distinct from a medical caffeine budget. [FDA on caffeine sources and amounts](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much).
