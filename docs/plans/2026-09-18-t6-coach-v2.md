## Why

Devin's three sentences are the acceptance test for the whole app:

- "Add watermelon to my avoid list."
- "Can you give me a recipe for a chicken wrap that fits my daily macros?"
- "Change my 4th meal to beef jerky and a Chobani yogurt."

Today (recon 2026-09-18, `lib/server/coach.ts`, `lib/coach/*`): the coach has **no memory** (each
turn sends the system prompt, the tool spec and her one new message), **no context** (today's date
in the server's tz and her constraint profile; no tool reads the plan slots, the eaten state, the
entries or her lists — `get_day` returns totals only), **no avoid-list action**, **no recipe or
compose tool**, and its actions edit a slot item by item. So sentence 1 is impossible, sentence 2
is a paragraph of prose with no card, and sentence 3 needs three actions and a slot the coach
cannot see. The plumbing that exists is good and stays: server-side grounding through
`searchFoods` + the policy, client-side apply through the same write paths she uses by hand,
action cards with Undo, idempotent turns, the injection corpus (F-098), the circuit breaker.

## Design

### 1. Memory — the thread remembers

- Every turn sends, after the system prompt and before her message: the thread's rolling
  **summary** (≤ 600 characters, stored in a new column `coach_threads.summary text`), then the
  last **12 messages** verbatim (user and assistant `content`; assistant action cards rendered as
  one line each, e.g. `[action: set_slot_meal meal-4 → beef jerky 40 g, Chobani 150 g]`),
  trimmed from the oldest until the window is ≤ 6 000 tokens by the adapter's estimate.
- The final envelope gains an optional `memory` string (≤ 600 chars): the model's own updated
  summary of what matters (her stated preferences, decisions, open questions). The server stores
  it on the thread when present. No second model call.
- Resume: `getThread` already restores the messages; the summary rides with the thread so a
  reload or a new device continues with the same memory.
- Migration `supabase/migrations/<ts>_coach_thread_summary.sql` (+ `.down.sql`): the one column.

### 2. Context — the coach sees her day

Injected every turn as fenced tool-result data (the existing `renderToolResult` fencing, so it is
data and never instructions), built server-side from the synced tables, for **her local date**:

- **Her clock**: `now` (ISO) and `tz` (IANA) come from the client in the POST body (zod, both
  required; `DEFAULT_TZ` is the fallback only when the body has none). Every date the coach
  reasons about, and every `at` it emits, is in her tz.
- **Today's plan**: each slot in order with its label, planned time, items (name, grams, macros),
  and whether it is logged (`eaten`), pending or skipped. Source: `plan_days` for the date, the
  rotation's resolved meals, and the day's `log_entries` (the same resolution `app/page.tsx`
  does, moved into a server module `lib/server/day-context.ts` so both use it).
- **Today's entries**: every logged entry with its time, slot or "extra", items and macros.
- **Targets and what is still to eat**: kcal, protein, carbs, fat for the day type; the
  remainder after what is logged.
- **Her lists**: Eats foods and Avoid foods by name (from `user_food_prefs` + `user_foods` /
  catalog names), the constraint profile as today.
- **Her recipes**: name, per-serving macros, item names (`recipes` current revision).
- New tools `get_plan { date }` and `get_entries { date }` return the same shapes for another
  date. `get_day` is unchanged.
- The context block is capped at ~2 500 tokens; when a list overflows, the block says how many
  more there are and the tool exists for the rest.

### 3. Actions — the sentences she says

All actions keep the contract of F-119: grounded on the server (search + policy), **nothing
written on the server during the turn**, applied on the phone through the write paths she uses
by hand, shown as a card with Undo, idempotent by `actionId`. New kinds in `lib/coach/actions.ts`
(`ACTION_KINDS`), applied in `lib/coach/apply-client.ts` (split first — see §6):

| Action | Fields | Grounding | Applies through |
|---|---|---|---|
| `add_to_avoid` | `{ name }` | one `food_ref_id` via `searchFoods` (top-1; thin margin → one question) | the Avoid mark path `lib/foods/prefs-client.ts` (`status: "avoid"`), the same as the Foods tab toggle. Undo = the previous status. After apply, search, the scanner and the coach's own grounding see it (T1/T9). |
| `remove_from_avoid` | `{ name }` | as above | same path, `status: "eats"` or cleared |
| `set_slot_meal` | `{ date, slot, items: [{ name, grams? }] }` | every item grounded; a missing quantity → the catalog household portion, else one question; policy verdict per item, blocked items listed in `flagged` (marks, never refuses — ruling 2026-09-14) | the slot override path (`lib/plan/slot-override.ts` → `saveMealEdit`), replacing the whole slot's components. Undo = the previous override. |
| `log_meal` | gains `at` (HH:MM in her tz) and `slot: "snack"` (an extra, not a slot) | as today | `commitLogEntry` with `eaten_at` from `at` |
| `delete_entry` | `{ entryId }` (must be in the injected entries) | none | `forgetLoggedEntry` (T0's delete path) |
| `move_entry` | `{ entryId, at }` | none | the entry edit path (`saveExtraNote`-style update with `eaten_at`) |
| `save_recipe` | `{ name, items: [{ name, grams }] }` | every item grounded | `lib/recipes-client.ts` create; Undo = delete the revision |

"Change my 4th meal to beef jerky and a Chobani yogurt" is one `set_slot_meal` on the fourth
slot of her day (the coach maps "4th meal" through the injected slot order); Chobani is dairy,
so it lands with a `flagged` mark and the card says so, and she can still confirm. Slot names in
her words ("lunch", "meal 2", "the 4th one") resolve against the injected plan.

### 4. Compose — a real card, not a paragraph

- New tool `compose_meal { wish, slot?, servings? }` (effect: draft). The server builds a meal
  from her Eats foods and the catalog: it searches each food the wish names (chicken, wrap …),
  substitutes constraint-safe options when a named food is blocked (gluten → a gluten-free
  tortilla row if the catalog has one, else the flag is shown), sizes the grams to the slot's
  target or to what is still to eat today, and runs the policy. Result: `{ items: [{ name,
  food_ref_id, grams, macros }], totals, flagged, fitsTarget }`.
- The reply carries it as a **suggestion card** (the existing `CoachSuggestionCard`, extended)
  with three buttons: **Log it** (→ `log_meal` now, at her current time), **Put it in <slot>**
  (→ `set_slot_meal`), **Save as recipe** (→ `save_recipe`). Each is an ordinary action apply.
- "Give me a recipe for a chicken wrap that fits my daily macros" therefore returns a card with
  grams that add up to her remaining targets, and one tap saves it as a recipe.

### 5. Speed and resilience

- **Fallback model on 503**: when `gemini-3.6-flash` fails the retry budget, the same turn is
  retried once on `gemini-3.8-flash` (the capture model), recorded in `ai_calls.model_id`. The
  circuit is per provider as today.
- Streaming is **not** in this slice; `first-text` keeps being stamped with `reply-done` and the
  panel reports the p95 (AC8) without a pass/fail bar. The T0 deadlines and warm route stay.
- Budgets unchanged: `TURN_LIMITS` (10 tool calls, 75 s, 1 200 output tokens); the context
  block counts against the input budget the adapter already tracks.

### 6. Structure

- `lib/server/coach.ts` (618 lines) is split before anything is added: `lib/server/coach-turn.ts`
  (the loop), `lib/server/coach-threads.ts` (storage), `lib/server/coach-context.ts` (§2), the
  rest stays. `lib/coach/apply-client.ts` (409) splits into `apply-log.ts`, `apply-slot.ts`,
  `apply-prefs.ts` (§3), with `apply-client.ts` as the dispatcher. `components/CoachChat.tsx`
  (418): the suggestion card moves out. Every touched file ends ≤ 400 lines.
- `docs/features.md`: F-119 gains the new kinds; new rows for memory, context, compose and the
  fallback model, each naming its file; `pnpm lint:docs` stays clean. `docs/architecture.md`
  §4.5 gains the memory and context paragraphs.

## Acceptance criteria (the panel's, hidden from the coder)

Run against the live `/api/coach` route on the **local stack** with the real model (paid tier,
D1) and a seeded day: four slots with items and times, two logged entries, targets, an Eats list
containing chicken, banana and Chobani-free items, gluten/dairy/apple allergens on the profile.

- **AC1 Avoid list.** "Add watermelon to my avoid list" → one `add_to_avoid` card; after apply
  the Avoid list holds watermelon (`user_food_prefs.status = 'avoid'` on a resolved ref), a
  search for watermelon is blocked with the avoid reason, and the coach's next grounding of
  "watermelon" is flagged. Undo restores the previous status.
- **AC2 Replace a slot.** "Change my 4th meal to beef jerky and a Chobani yogurt" → one
  `set_slot_meal` on the fourth slot with both items grounded and grams from the household
  portion; Chobani carries the dairy flag on the card; after apply the slot shows the two items
  with their macros, survives a reload, and Undo restores the previous slot.
- **AC3 Compose.** "Give me a recipe for a chicken wrap that fits my daily macros" → a
  suggestion card whose items are policy-clean (no gluten wrap unless a gluten-free row exists;
  otherwise the flag is shown), whose totals are within 10 % of the remaining targets, with
  "Log it", "Put it in <slot>" and "Save as recipe"; "Save as recipe" creates a `recipes` row
  with those items; "Log it" logs the entry at her current time.
- **AC4 Memory.** A ten-turn conversation in one thread: turn 2 "make that 3 pieces of jerky"
  edits the same slot as turn 1; turn 9 "what did I ask you to avoid earlier?" names watermelon;
  after a reload the thread resumes and turn 11 still knows both; `coach_threads.summary` is
  non-empty and under 600 characters.
- **AC5 Context.** "What do I have left to eat today?" is answered from the injected context in
  ≤ 1 tool round with numbers equal to the seeded remainder ± 1 %; "what's my 3rd meal?" names
  the seeded third slot's items.
- **AC6 Her clock.** With the body's `tz` set to America/Denver and the server in UTC, "I ate
  eggs at 9 AM" logs `eaten_at` = 09:00 Denver; "move it to 1:30" moves that entry;
  "delete it" removes it. Each is one card with Undo.
- **AC7 No regression.** `pnpm test` once green, tsc, lint incl. lint:docs, design lint; the
  injection corpus (F-098) with the new tools and the context block planted as injection sites
  shows zero out-of-registry calls and no action against a food not in the reply's grounding;
  every touched file ≤ 400 lines; a turn still writes nothing on the server except the thread's
  messages and summary.
- **AC8 Speed (report, not a bar).** p50/p95 of `coach-turn` sent→reply-done and
  `coach-action` action-applied over the AC1–AC6 turns, from `client_timings`; the fallback model
  is exercised once by a stubbed 503 and the turn completes.
