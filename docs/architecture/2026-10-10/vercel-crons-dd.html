Vercel cron map

Ten crons call /api/jobs/* in sfo1. Every call checks the secret before the limiter. Four jobs walk all accounts through a resumable pass. The rest run once, hourly or in short loops.

When each cron fires

UTC axis on top, US Eastern (EDT, UTC-4) below. After 2026-11-01 Eastern is one hour earlier than shown. Source: vercel.json:4-13

What one call does

Gate order checked in all ten routes. Pass lease, budget and account lock apply to estimate, weekly-proposal, oura-pull and facet-warm.

Time

Budget and function limit

  • Nine jobs set maxDuration = 300. spend-sweep sets 90 and stops at 60 s.
  • No account starts after 240 s. In-flight work is dropped and queued at 285 s. job-cursor.ts:202-203
  • The pass lease lasts 360 s, longer than any run.
  • All functions run in sfo1. vercel.json:2

Failure

Who retries what

  • A failed account gets an error lock and joins the pass retry list. The next fire retries it.
  • weekly-review retries a week once, then stops.
  • backup fires once a day. A failure waits for the next day.
  • The cursor jobs answer 200 even when accounts failed.

Access

Service role only

  • All ten use adminClient(). None runs as a user.
