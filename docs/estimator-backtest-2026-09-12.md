## §4.3 acceptance claims

1a. Artefact bounded — a 1.5 kg water swing over 3 days moves TDEE by at most 5 % against a
    matched, artefact-free control (same seed, artefact only) and changes no status.
1b. Cycle artefact bounded at the measured rate — a ±0.8 kg 28-day cycle moves TDEE by at most
    21.4 % (restated 2026-09-12, ruling 10; architecture.md §4.3) and changes no status.
2. Under-logging holds — the under-logging row's statuses must contain `held` and no run after
   day 90 may report a TDEE below the last `estimating` value.
3. Real change detected — the +250 kcal step must land within ±100 kcal of 2380 by
   day 81, and the ramp must stay under 120 kcal of truth.

## Reading the results

- **1a water swing** — the 5.4 % measured against the global steady-state control was dominated
  by the cross-seed noise floor of the comparison, not by the artefact. Resolved by ruling 10
  (2026-09-12, contracts §7): this claim now compares against a matched control (same LCG seed,
  artefact only), which isolates the artefact and measures the pct in the table above — under
  the 5 % bound.
- **1b 28-day cycle** — the EMA passes a 28-day antiphase cycle straight into `tdee_raw`; at
  alpha = 2/21 the EMA's gain at a 28-day period is ≈ 0.41 and trend[t] − trend[t−14] is antiphase
  at that period, so 5 % is unreachable at today's alpha and prior clamp. Resolved by ruling 10
  (2026-09-12, contracts §7): the bound is restated to the measured rate (architecture.md §4.3);
  a second smoother or a tighter prior clamp are follow-up contract changes, not done here.
- **2 under-logging** — resolved by the one-way burn-in latch: once the lifetime gate is earned
  a thin last-21 holds the last estimate instead of re-entering `burn_in`. No constant changed.

Measured by `tests/expenditure/backtest.test.ts`. No constant is tuned to make a bound pass; a
bound that cannot be met is restated with the measured figure and the reason, never silently
lowered (contracts §7).
